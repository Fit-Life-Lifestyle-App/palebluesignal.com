About Pale Blue Signal

Pale Blue Signal is written by Daniel Johnson. Each post covers one story in about two minutes: what happened, why it matters, and where it’s headed. Every fact links to its source, and when a story develops, the post gets a dated update at the top so you can see what changed.

Posts are drafted with AI help from Daniel’s own notes and opinions. The audio versions use an AI version of his voice.
